# Northwind Cloud — careers notes

## Our values

- **Ownership** — we hire engineers who take problems end-to-end and see them through.
- **Customers first** — every decision starts from what helps the people using our platform.
- **Bias for action** — we value momentum; a good decision shipped today beats a perfect one next quarter.
- **Reliability as a feature** — we believe correctness and uptime are part of the product, not ops chores.

## What we look for

We value engineers who reason about caching and reliability, and who can explain
the trade-offs they made. Our interview process is a technical deep-dive loop:
expect practical questions, a system-design round, and behavioral interviews
grounded in our values.
